Backup
No suitable hero available? Send troops without a hero rather than using a weaker first choice. For joining, only the first Expedition skill of the first hero contributes a hero buff. Season 3 heroes such as Petra are useful when hosting, but do not put one first when joining just because it is newer; use a proven first-skill damage buff.

Before and during the trap

Make every march count.

01

Use the right buff

Activate an available attack or lethality pet buff just before Bear Trap. A defense-only buff does not improve Bear damage.

02

Fill strong rallies first

Prioritize the alliance’s strongest rally leaders. For this alliance’s current plan, send no more than 100K troops when joining so more members can fit; follow any updated officer cap.

03

Start your own rally

Open one even if only one troop is available after joining stronger rallies. Your rally gives other members another place to join.
