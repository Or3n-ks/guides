Fast ore strategy

Eternity’s Reach

Keep marches moving, unlock the ore skill quickly, and switch to Fractured Veins when they appear.

Opening and skill tree

Hunt Cesares. Build R – R – L – L – R.

Attack Cesares continuously at the start to earn ore and unlock skills. Keep hunting with free marches throughout the match, especially while the skill tree is still growing.

Skill order: Right → Right → Left → Left → Right. Activate each unlocked buff and use it deliberately. The third choice unlocks a 5,000 Copper bonus for occupying a common vein, with a one-minute cooldown. The final active gathering buff applies to marches sent after you activate it.

The ore loop

  • 1After the third skill unlocks, send a march into a nearby common vein. Collect the 5,000 Copper entry bonus, then recall. Re-enter when the one-minute cooldown is ready. Do not leave a march sitting in one vein for minutes just for slow gathering.
  • 2When Fractured Veins appear, switch immediately and contest them. The 5,000 Copper entry bonus is for common veins; treat Fractured Veins as a separate high-value burst.
  • 3Try the Peak of Eternity if you can hold it. Strong players should contest it. If a stronger player knocks you out, teleport to a quieter area and resume Cesare hunts and the vein loop.
