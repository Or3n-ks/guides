Combat reserve: keep enough RSS and healing speedups for the fight itself. A strong Prep score helps, but it does not replace attendance, coordination, garrisons, rallies, or disciplined healing.

Simple rule

  • ✓Start saving rare resources before Prep begins.
  • ✓Use each item on its best scoring day instead of spending everything on Day 1.
  • ✓Coordinate kingdom pushes so the strongest accounts peak together.
