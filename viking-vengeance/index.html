Alliance defense guide

Viking Vengeance

Empty your city, reinforce active allies, and move assigned marches to HQ for waves 10 and 20.

Before wave 1

Make room for reinforcements.

  • 1Send your Infantry and Cavalry to reinforce alliance members. Empty your city as much as your march capacity allows; if troops must stay home, leave Archers. Fewer home troops means allies reinforcing you can take more kills.
  • 2Spread your marches across several allies when one city cannot hold them all. Prefer online members whose cities are ready to receive reinforcements.
  • 3Place your three strongest suitable defenders in the Guard Station. Use Howard as the lead for this setup when he is among your best defenders; a stronger later-generation defensive lead may replace him.

During the waves

2×

After two burns

Vikings stop targeting that city. Move your reinforcements to other cities that can still receive attacks; the burned player can keep earning points by reinforcing others.

ON

Stay online

Waves 7, 14, and 17 target online players. Offline cities are skipped for these waves, so defend active members and stay available to redirect marches.

HQ

Waves 10 and 20

These waves hit Alliance HQ. Follow the HQ captain's assignment and send the designated march after waves 9 and 19 have fully ended.

Timing: keep the HQ march there until wave 10 finishes, then return it to city defense before wave 11. Repeat the move for wave 20. Wave 20 ends the event; there is no wave 21.
