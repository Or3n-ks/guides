“Do” means complete the Intel mission. “Claim” means collect its reward. Complete Sunday’s 08:00 batch without claiming it; leave the 16:00 batch unfinished until Monday.

Pan’s extra missions

  • 1On Sunday, wait until after 16:00 UTC to activate Pan’s extra Intel missions. Leave them unfinished.
  • 2After Monday’s 00:00 UTC kingdom reset, complete and claim Sunday’s held missions and the new Intel batch. Check the expiry timers on the held missions.
  • 3Activate Pan again on Monday for a new daily set. That gives you Sunday’s held Pan set plus Monday’s new set.
The payoff: Monday begins with an unclaimed 08:00 batch, an unfinished 16:00 batch, new 00:00 missions, and two Pan sets. Claiming the saved rewards during Golden Glaives adds Dim Goldstones; completing held missions after reset can also count for a concurrent Intel-scoring event.

During the event, Golden Glaive hunts are another source of Dim Goldstones. Exchange your Goldstones before the redemption window closes.
