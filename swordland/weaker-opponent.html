Swordland matchup plan

When We Are Stronger

Convert the advantage into early point income and defend what we bank. Do not spend the hour chasing cities while valuable buildings are empty.

Opening: 0–15 min

Take the map without overextending.

Move active building owners and strike players forward when shorter marches help secure the next objective. Joiners whose marches arrive on time can keep their towns in the safe zone; see the positioning rule.

  • 1Run the four-squad opening: both Sanctums, Bell Tower, and Stables. Strike helps whichever first-control fight is contested.
  • 2After owners report SET, use spare marches to occupy Abbeys. Keep the Sanctum garrisons strong; an enemy can reclaim banked relics from a weakly held building.
  • 3Collectors sweep every building flip. City attacks are useful only if they protect or open a named objective.

Center unlock

Take Hall and Swordshrine.

Bell squad leads Hall. The Stables squad provides the Swordshrine garrison leader; strike group takes the fight. Keep secondary marches in Bell and Stables only if they will hold. Preserve both Sanctum cores.

Do not deliberately vacate Swordshrine late. If we are winning and can hold it, continued occupation plus protection of our bank is worth more than a risky last-second recapture.

Endgame

Close the match instead of farming fights.

WhenOrder
8 min leftCalculate whether existing point income protects the lead. Reinforce the buildings with the largest banks. Position collectors for an enemy flip.
5 min leftStop routine healing exits. Rally only an enemy building whose bank or buff materially increases the lead without weakening our anchors.
Final 2 minPrioritize preventing flips and collecting exposed drops. No unassigned chase or empty handoff.

Switch conditions

If their best garrison begins beating ours or our point income falls below theirs, switch to the even-match plan. If repeated rallies fail and anchors cannot be held, switch to the stronger-opponent plan.
