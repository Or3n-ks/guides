Swordland matchup plan

When They Are Stronger

Do not feed the opponent repeated failed rallies. Keep earning reachable points, preserve active marches, and prepare a late strike only where the score bank can change the outcome.

Opening: 0–15 min

Claim what we can actually hold.

Try the two Sanctums and early buffs, but move off a building after clear evidence their main defender dominates it. Reassign that squad to a reachable Abbey, collection, or reinforcement. First-control points matter; handing a growing bank back matters more.

  • 1Keep most towns in the safe zone, including strong rally leaders when their march times work. Move only active responders needed for a timed objective; keep forward towns empty and extinguish fires promptly. See the positioning rule.
  • 2Use the strike group on isolated or underfilled buildings, not the opponent's strongest garrison. Read every report.
  • 3Collectors track drops and later Undercellars. Gather with spare queues, and recall for a called attack.

Center unlock

Do not tunnel on Swordshrine.

If Hall is lightly defended, take its combat buff. If the enemy commits most of its best marches to Swordshrine, attack their weaker flank and collect there. A late Swordshrine ambush is valid only if our best rally has a credible path through its garrison and our collectors can reach the drops.

20,000 visible bank: That is accumulated points above the building, not a guaranteed reward to our side. The opponent can lose it when the building flips, and either alliance may collect the packages. Plan the attack and collection together.

Endgame

Prepare one credible comeback.

WhenOrder
8 min leftCalculate the gap and identify the largest bank our rally can realistically reach. If no building can close it, maximize attainable alliance and personal points rather than wasting the final marches.
6–7 min leftStage rally leader, extra joiners, a landing garrison, and collectors. Keep at least one defensible anchor staffed.
~5 min leftLaunch for full control. Do not depend on a two-minute-left rally unless it is an explicit last-chance loot gamble.
On impactCollect immediately. If the first rally fails, pivot to the fallback instead of repeating it without a changed matchup.

Switch conditions

If their best marches become depleted or leave buildings exposed, move to the even-match plan. Judge that from current reports, not pre-match power.
