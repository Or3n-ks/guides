Swordland matchup plan

When the Match Is Even

Hold two dependable anchors and concentrate the remaining power. The winning move is usually one well-timed building flip with collectors ready, not attacks scattered across the map.

Opening: 0–15 min

Protect the two Sanctums.

Keep most joiners and the caller in the safe zone. Move only the leaders and collectors whose shorter marches change the current fight; see the positioning rule.

Run the four-squad opening, then use the strike group to resolve one contested building at a time. A utility building is helpful; a Sanctum left without a garrison is costly. Assign its owner before the first march lands.

  • 1When an enemy building is weak, take it with a named garrison and collectors ready.
  • 2When a rally fails, read the report. Change leader, joiners, or target instead of repeating the same loss.
  • 3Keep enough spare marches to reinforce an anchor. Do not lock every queue in Undercellars.

Center unlock

Test, then commit or pivot.

Bell squad tries for Hall. Strike and Stables squads test Swordshrine together. If the first fight indicates we can take and hold it, commit the named defender. If their garrison is too strong, stop and protect both Sanctums while the strike group targets an exposed building or a large visible bank.

Decision gate: Score gain from a flip must justify any Sanctum troops borrowed for it. The Swordshrine is the highest-value point building, but losing two anchors to chase it can lose the match.

Endgame

Attack the bank that closes the gap.

WhenOrder
8 min leftCompare score gap, point rates, visible banks, garrison reports, and travel time. Pick one primary target and one fallback.
6–7 min leftStage strike leader, joiners, landing garrison, and collectors. Free needed marches from gathering.
~5 min leftLaunch if full occupation is required. A one-minute rally begun with two minutes left is only a risky attempt to trigger and collect drops.
After the flipCollect every package and hold the building. If the attack fails, use the fallback immediately.

Switch conditions

If we establish a stable point-rate lead, use the stronger-side close. If their leader cannot be beaten and we are losing anchors, use the stronger-opponent plan.
